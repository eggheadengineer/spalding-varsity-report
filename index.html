Won at Mount St. Joseph

Seven wins in seven games, after going 9‑10 last season. On Nov 9 they won 6–3 at Mount St. Joseph, which was unbeaten and had beaten Spalding 3 of 4 times last season. Spalding trailed 2–0 and 3–2, then scored the last four goals, two of them on one power play. Against private‑school teams they're 4‑0, outscoring them 19–8, and two of those wins came over teams that beat them last year: St. John's (6–2) and Calvert Hall (3–0, the team that ended their season in the "A" semifinal). The other three wins came against public schools (Broadneck 10–0, Severna Park 7–0, South River 1–0). Shots have evened out: outshot 37–20 at St. John's, then 26–25 against Calvert Hall and 23–22 at Mount St. Joseph. Monday was a MAPHL game, not an MIAA conference game; the two conference games against Mount St. Joseph are Dec 11 and Jan 25.

Latest games

Nov 9, at Mount St. Joseph: W 6–3. A MAPHL AA game. Cole Zoren scored twice in the first half of the 1st for Mount St. Joseph, both from Eric Bieda and Luke Phelan, and Michael McGuire got one back on the power play late in the period (from Gloman and Hong). Christian Gloman tied it 2–2 early in the 2nd (from Stanley Wasielewski and Patrick McGuire), and Jackson Crigger put Mount St. Joseph back ahead on the power play. Midway through the 2nd, Phelan took a 5‑minute major for checking from behind plus a misconduct, and Spalding scored twice on it, 31 seconds apart: Grant Christiansen (from Sawyer Fox and Logan Rich) tied it, and McGuire (from Rocha and Gloman) got the winner. Wasielewski scored twice in 54 seconds early in the 3rd to make it 6–3. Spalding outshot them 23–22. Max Grant made 19 saves on 22 shots. Mount St. Joseph took 8 penalties, including majors on Phelan and Bieda, its two leading goal scorers.

Nov 5, vs Calvert Hall: W 3–0. The MIAA "A" opener, and a MAPHL AA game too. Scoreless through the 1st and most of the 2nd. Lucas Rocha scored on the power play with 1:33 left in the 2nd (from Grant Christiansen and Christian Gloman), and Logan Rich made it 2–0 with 10 seconds left in the period (from Rocha). Rich scored again early in the 3rd (from Rocha and Michael McGuire). Shots were 26–25. Max Grant made all 25 saves. Calvert Hall beat Spalding 4–3 in last season's "A" semifinal.

Nov 4, at Loyola Blakefield: W 4–3. Only the score is posted. Mount St. Joseph needed overtime to beat Loyola by the same score the day before.

Nov 3, at St. John's College: W 6–2 (exhibition). Down 0–2 by the middle of the 1st. Patrick McGuire scored on the power play late in the 1st, then Spalding scored three in the 2nd (Gloman, and Michael McGuire twice, once on the power play) and two in the 3rd. Michael McGuire finished with a hat trick, and Kyle Hong had a goal and 3 assists. Outshot 37–20. Preston Vandercloff played the first half and gave up both goals in the 1st; Max Grant played the second half and stopped everything (13 of 13 in the 3rd). St. John's beat Spalding 8–0 and 4–1 last season.
